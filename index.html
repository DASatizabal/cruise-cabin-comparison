About these prices

Prices come from Norwegian Cruise Line's booking system and are checked once a day. They change often, so check NCL before booking.

Each total is the cruise fare, NCL's government taxes, fees and port expenses, the daily service charges (crew gratuities) for every guest 3 and older when you prepay them (otherwise they're shown separately as paid onboard), the gratuities for the Free at Sea offers you keep switched on, and Free at Sea Plus if you choose it. Unlimited Open Bar: guests 21 and over pay the full gratuity, a guest under 21 who is guest 2 pays for a soda package instead, and other guests under 21 pay nothing. Specialty Dining: guests 13 and over pay, under 13 eat free. NCL lists adults first, then kids oldest first. If NCL doesn't show a number on a given day, this page says so instead of guessing.

Florida resident rates aren't on NCL's public website. They are read from NCL once a week while signed in to a Florida resident account ("Florida rates as of" at the top). When NCL offers a discount, each cabin's own amount comes off the fare for guests 1 and 2 (for 8 nights, each ship's own amount). Cabins not checked, or not checked in the last 15 days, show NCL's website price. Switch it off to see NCL's published prices.
