About these prices

Prices come from Norwegian Cruise Line's booking system and are checked once a day. They change often, so check NCL before booking.

Each total is the cruise fare, NCL's government taxes, fees and port expenses, and the Free at Sea gratuities. Unlimited Open Bar: guests 21 and over pay the full gratuity, a guest under 21 who is guest 2 pays for a soda package instead, and other guests under 21 pay nothing. Specialty Dining: guests 13 and over pay, under 13 eat free. NCL lists adults first, then kids oldest first.
